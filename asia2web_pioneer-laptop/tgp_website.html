Who We Are

About the Fraternity

Tau Gamma Phi – Triskelion Grand Fraternity is one of the most respected and influential fraternities in the Philippines. Founded on the principles of brotherhood, loyalty, and service, we have shaped communities and produced leaders across all sectors of society.

Our fraternity is built on three pillars represented by our Triskelion symbol: Academic Excellence, Community Service, and Brotherhood. These values guide every Triskelion in their personal and professional lives.

Locally, our chapter is dedicated to uplifting the community through meaningful programs, outreach initiatives, and active civic participation — proving that true brotherhood extends beyond our ranks.

Our Legacy

History & Milestones

1968

The Founding

Tau Gamma Phi was established with a vision of brotherhood and national service. The Triskelion symbol was chosen to represent the three core values of the fraternity.

1975

National Expansion

The fraternity expanded across major universities in the Philippines, establishing chapters that upheld the same founding principles of honor and service.

1990

Community Programs Launched

The fraternity formalized its community outreach initiatives, beginning structured programs for education, livelihood, and social welfare in local communities.

2005

Local Chapter Established

Our local chapter was officially founded, bringing the Triskelion spirit to our community. Charter members pledged to serve with honor and integrity.

2015

Free Breakfast Program

Launched the community Free Breakfast Program to address hunger among underprivileged families, serving hundreds of meals monthly to those in need.

2020

Clean-Up Drive Initiative

Initiated regular community clean-up drives promoting environmental responsibility and civic pride across our barangay and surrounding areas.

2024

Today & Beyond

Our chapter continues to grow stronger, welcoming new brothers while deepening our roots in community service, academic excellence, and genuine brotherhood.

Community Service

Our Projects

🌿
Environment

Community Clean-Up Drive

Every month, our brothers gather to clean our community's streets, esteros, and public spaces. We believe a clean community reflects a disciplined brotherhood. Participants also engage with residents to promote proper waste segregation and environmental awareness.

🍳
Social Welfare

Free Breakfast Program

No child or family should start their day hungry. Our Free Breakfast Program provides hot, nutritious meals to underprivileged members of our community every week. Brothers personally prepare and serve the food, building genuine connections with those we serve.

📚
Education

Youth Mentorship

Our brothers serve as mentors and role models to the youth in our community, offering academic assistance, life skills coaching, and moral guidance. We invest in the next generation because they are the future of our nation.
